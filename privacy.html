PRIVACY NOTICE

The Hoon Collective (THC) is an informal Australian automotive club based in Albury–Wodonga. This notice explains how we intend to handle information submitted through our membership application process.

Contact: thehooncollective@outlook.com

Information we collect

When applications open, the form will request your legal name, preferred name (optional), date of birth, email address, phone number, suburb or town, state or territory, vehicle details (optional), automotive interests and reason for joining. We also record your acceptance of membership declarations and your application status.

Why we collect it

We use application details to assess eligibility (including the minimum age of 18), contact you about your application, manage prospect and membership decisions, and administer the club. Do not include sensitive information in free-text answers.

Where information is handled

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Application submissions are intended to be processed through Supabase and stored in a private Supabase database. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for automated activity. These services may process technical information such as IP addresses and security signals under their own terms and privacy practices.

Who can access it

Application records are intended to be accessible only to authorised club administrators with appropriate Supabase access, and to service providers as needed to operate the application system. We do not publish applicants' personal details or sell membership application information.

Retention and deletion

Unsuccessful (rejected) or withdrawn membership applications are scheduled for deletion 90 calendar days after the final decision or withdrawal, unless a legal obligation or legitimate administrative need requires longer retention. Pending applications are reviewed separately; this 90-day period does not start until a final decision or withdrawal. Approved members' records are managed separately for ongoing membership administration, and their retention schedule will be documented before public applications open. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing us. Certain records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate administration needs.

Security

We use server-side checks, access restrictions, bot verification and submission rate limits. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or identity documents through the application form.

Questions or complaints

Email thehooncollective@outlook.com with privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or complaints. We will review your enquiry and respond as reasonably practicable.

Last updated: 22 October 2026. This notice may be revised as club processes develop.
