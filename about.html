Our story

ABOUT THC

The Hoon Collective is an Australian automotive community founded in 2026, starting in the Albury–Wodonga border region.

Every build has a place

We're here for people who love cars, bikes, 4WDs, restoration, modification, motorsport, photography and the friendships that come with automotive culture. All makes and models are welcome.

Where we're going

Our goal is to grow from a local Border Chapter into a nationwide community with organised meets, member projects and responsible events.

Our values

Respect, inclusion, safety and genuine enthusiasm. Competitive driving and burnouts belong at authorised venues, not on public roads during club activities.
